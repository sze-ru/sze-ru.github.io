JADE ONG — PERSONAL BRANDING WEBSITE
1. Project overview
Project: Personal branding / portfolio website
Client: Jade Ong
Website type: Personal portfolio and professional landing page
Purpose: To establish a strong personal brand, showcase my professional experience and selected work, and create a platform for future career opportunities.
I want a website that communicates my versatility across VIP hospitality, nightlife operations, event experience and creative marketing.
The website should feel like a creative portfolio rather than a traditional corporate resume.
2. Creative direction
Overall aesthetic: Dark editorial, modern nightlife-inspired, minimal but visually striking.
Colour palette:
- Primary: Black / charcoal
- Accent: Deep red / vibrant red
- Secondary: Chrome / metallic silver
- Text: White / light grey
Visual references and mood:
- High-end nightlife and hospitality branding
- Editorial fashion campaigns
- Contemporary luxury branding
- Chrome typography and metallic textures
- Subtle ambient lighting and gradients
The website should feel:
- Confident and distinctive
- Modern and slightly edgy
- Sophisticated, not overly corporate
- Nightlife-inspired without looking like a nightclub promotional page
Avoid excessive neon effects, cluttered layouts, generic corporate templates and overly decorative animations.
