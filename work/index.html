2023 — Present

Nightlife & hospitality.

Personal portrait · Waterfront setting

Nightlife has taught me to pay attention before I speak: to read the room, recognise what a guest needs and adjust how I communicate. When plans change or the pace picks up, I have to think clearly, solve problems with the team and stay composed. Working across different venues continues to strengthen my adaptability and confidence, while reminding me that a memorable experience is built on trust, discretion and consistent care.

My Role

VIP hosting · Bottle service · Guest relations · Brand ambassadorship · Event staffing · Operations

What I Contributed

  • I continue to work across VIP hosting and bottle service, guest relations and brand ambassadorship, hospitality and event staffing, and venue coordination.
  • Across clubs, lounges, karaoke venues and live events, I adapt my approach to the setting and the people in front of me.
  • I build rapport with VIP guests, support table management and deliver bottle presentations and premium beverage service.
  • Event and promotional assignments give me experience representing brands and welcoming guests beyond regular venue service.
  • My previous administrative and operational responsibilities included staff scheduling and allocation, claims administration, training coordination and venue support.

What I Took Away

Good service depends on what happens behind the scenes as much as what a guest sees. Clear communication with floor, bar and security teams helps me respond to requests, manage pressure and keep the guest experience consistent.

Content production / Events

WaterSource Evergreen × ELDEX

Working with WaterSource Evergreen took me through the process of creating content for a real client: understanding the products, proposing ideas, planning shoots and refining the finished work. Site visits and conversations with the founder helped me connect our content choices to the people and purpose behind the brand.

My Role

Product content · Filming · Event coverage · Editing · Client presentations

What I Contributed

  • Helped plan and produce product videos, with site visits, filming, founder interview preparation and filming support.
  • Supported event setup and filmed across all three days of ELDEX Asia 2026, adapting around visitors, booth activities and changing schedules.
  • Created event content and worked on highlight videos and post-event edits through multiple rounds of client feedback.
  • Contributed content ideas, including Guess-the-Product, and prepared material for client presentations.

What I Took Away

In Week 3, pitching to the client showed me that an idea I found exciting still needed to suit the brand and be practical to deliver. By Weeks 4 and 5, I was learning how much planning sits behind a short video: the shots, pacing and message all matter. Filming at ELDEX required me to respond to what was happening around me, while a challenging customer interaction reminded me to stay calm and professional. Revising the highlights through Week 7 taught me to listen carefully, rethink work when needed and treat feedback as part of the creative process.

For Melissa Shoes, I supported the research and coordination behind brand content. The work connected audience insights and creative ideas with the practical details of finding talents, preparing briefings and helping the team get ready to shoot.

My Role

Brand research · Social media analysis · Talent coordination · Content planning

What I Contributed

  • Researched the brand and analysed TikTok and Instagram content to understand its audience and visual direction.
  • Researched potential talents, carried out outreach and helped prepare briefings and collect reference photos and videos.
  • Developed content ideas and presentation slides, refining the proposed direction with the team.
  • Supported preparations for photoshoot and video content, connecting talent information with the creative plan.

What I Took Away

My Week 6 reflection made me recognise that creative work depends on organisation and follow-through. Gathering talent information, sharing references and finishing a presentation on time were small tasks with a direct effect on the team’s next steps. Continuing the outreach and presentation work in Week 7 helped me become more deliberate about priorities and clearer in how I communicate. I am learning to balance enthusiasm for an idea with the coordination needed to make it happen.

Reflections & learning

Learning as the
work unfolds.

Adapted from my weekly LinkedIn reflections, these moments show how my understanding of creative work and responsibility has developed.

  1. Week 1

    Finding my footing

    Joining a new team meant adapting to different working styles and unfamiliar challenges. I wanted to put classroom learning into practice while becoming a better communicator and teammate.

  2. Week 3

    Listening before moving forward

    Presenting to WaterSource Evergreen challenged me to explain why our ideas would work. Client feedback helped me see that confidence includes being willing to change my mind.

  3. Week 4

    Making the idea tangible

    Site visits, filming and the founder interview showed me the thought behind each shot. I began connecting storytelling, pacing and key messages with the purpose of the content.

  4. Week 5

    Responding in the moment

    At ELDEX, filming plans had to fit the crowd and booth activity. I practised staying calm through unexpected situations and became more willing to rethink an edit instead of defending the first version.

  5. Week 6

    Making room for every responsibility

    Balancing WaterSource, Melissa and DBM work pushed me to prioritise tasks and keep information moving. I saw how research, briefings and timely updates help the whole team make progress.

  6. Week 7

    Taking greater ownership

    The D’Studio handover brought new responsibilities. Completing my midpoint reflection helped me recognise progress, identify areas to improve and carry those lessons into the next part of TEP.

What I’m carrying forward

I want to bring more intention to the way I work: clarify the purpose before creating, communicate early when plans change and leave enough time for revisions. I am becoming more confident working with different people, while learning that initiative also means listening, supporting the team and taking responsibility for the details.
