02 / Content production / Events

2026

WaterSource Evergreen
× ELDEX Asia 2026.

A hands-on content project for WaterSource Evergreen, from client conversations and product filming to on-site coverage at ELDEX Asia 2026.

My role

Content ideation · Filming · Editing revisions · Client presentations

What I contributed

  • Contributed to the production of three marketing videos and prepared slides for client discussions.
  • Helped with event setup and filmed across the three-day ELDEX event.
  • Worked through feedback and editing revisions to refine product content and event highlights.

Reflection

Challenge

Our initial ELDEX ideas felt exciting, but client feedback challenged whether they suited the brand. During the event, crowds and booth activities changed our filming schedule; a difficult customer interaction also tested my composure.

How I responded

I listened to the client’s perspective, worked through repeated video revisions and adjusted filming around what was happening on the floor. At the booth, I stayed calm and handled the interaction professionally.

What I’ll carry forward

Before pushing an idea forward, I want to check what it communicates for the brand. I’ll make room for feedback and keep filming plans flexible enough for a live event.

03 / Creative marketing / Talent coordination

2026

Melissa Shoes.

Project cover / 2026Melissa Shoes

Talent coordination
& content planning

Supporting the people, planning and research behind brand content for Melissa Shoes through my Teaching Enterprise Project.

My role

Talent outreach · Content planning · Social media analysis

What I contributed

  • Reached out to talents, coordinated briefings and consolidated reference materials.
  • Researched content ideas and developed presentation slides aligned with the brand and its audience.
  • Analysed Instagram and TikTok posts and Reels performance to better understand audience response.

Reflection

Challenge

Melissa’s talent outreach and content deck ran alongside WaterSource edits and DBM filming. Keeping several deliverables moving challenged my time management.

How I responded

I broke down tasks and prioritised what needed completing first. I gathered talent information and reference material, coordinated with teammates and consolidated our research into slides within the deadline.

What I’ll carry forward

I want to prioritise deadlines more deliberately and make sure teammates have the information they need. Organisation is part of the creative work, especially when several projects overlap.
